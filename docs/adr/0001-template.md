# NNNN. Short title in imperative form

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names / handles

## Context

What's the situation? What forces (technical, product, time, team) are at play? Link the PRD section, the architecture rule being deviated from, or the bug that prompted this. One or two paragraphs.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? State it as a directive: *"We will X"*, *"Y is replaced by Z"*. Be concrete enough that a future reader can verify the codebase actually reflects this.

## Alternatives considered

Briefly: what else did we look at, and why did we reject each? One bullet per alternative is fine. Skip this section if there genuinely was only one option (and explain why in *Context*).

## Consequences

Both directions:

- **Positive:** what we gain.
- **Negative / costs:** what this commits us to, what becomes harder, what we can't easily change later.
- **Neutral but worth knowing:** follow-up work, migration steps, things to watch.
